# Level and Badge Rules

## Level progression

- `Beginner`: 0 XP, starter analytics, foundation plans, base coaching
- `Intermediate`: 750 XP, 5-day streak, 70 form average, 1 completed goal
- `Pro`: 1800 XP, 12-day streak, 82 form average, 2 completed goals
- `Athlete`: 3200 XP, 21-day streak, 90 form average, 4 completed goals

Progression is not label-only. Unlock bundles are tied to real metrics and power features.

## Quest system

- Daily workout completion quest
- Daily hydration goal quest
- Weekly consistency quest

Quest completion grants XP through the derived-state pipeline.
